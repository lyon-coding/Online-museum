A virtual museum of modern painting · 1860–1930

From Impression to Abstraction

How does one movement become another? What are the roots of each new way of painting?

Walk from Manet and the Impressionists, through Seurat, Cézanne, Van Gogh and Gauguin, to the Fauves, Cubism and the first abstract paintings of Kandinsky, Mondrian and Malevich. Every artist has a room. Doors behind you lead to their roots, doors ahead to the painters who followed, and side doors to contemporaries of the same movement.

On a computer

  • Drag to look around
  • W A S D or arrows to walk, Shift to walk faster
  • Click a painting to enlarge it, or the floor to walk there
  • T timeline · I room info · Esc close

On a phone or tablet

  • Drag to look around
  • Use the joystick (bottom left), or tap the floor to walk there
  • Tap a painting to enlarge it, tap a door to see where it leads
